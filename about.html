00:04:00:00 — My Story

From Weekend Edits
To Full-Time Craft

I started editing videos two years ago as a hobby — helping a friend cut his YouTube uploads on weekends.

Somewhere along the way, what started as a favor turned into an obsession. I fell in love with storytelling — not just cutting clips together, but shaping pace, sound design, and color to build something that actually holds a viewer's attention. That obsession eventually became a full-time career.

What makes me different is simple: I don't just trim footage into a timeline. I build a narrative — a reason for the viewer to keep watching until the very last second. Every cut, transition, and sound cue is there to serve that story.

Mission

To help creators and brands turn raw, unorganized footage into polished stories that grow their audience.
